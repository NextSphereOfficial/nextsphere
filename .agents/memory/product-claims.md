The user describes NextSphere's behavior as: “l'AI non risponde a ciò che non sa, e appena il guest lo richiede, invia i contatti dell'host”.

**Why:** The audit suggested human escalation wording, but automatic forwarding or host notifications were not confirmed.

**How to apply:** Explain that the assistant does not invent answers and supplies host contact details when the guest requests them. Do not turn this into a promise of automatic hand-off or alerts.

The user confirmed Stripe is the payment processor.

**Why:** The user provided this product fact when discussing payment trust copy.

**How to apply:** Name Stripe accurately; the user's tentative “il più sicuro al mondo (presumo)” is not evidence for a security superlative.

Do not introduce new discounts or plans to satisfy the audit. Preserve the approved pricing and conversion mechanics, and do not publish invented testimonials.

**Why:** The user agreed to these boundaries and explicitly said not to insert testimonials without authentic material.

**How to apply:** Use verified product facts and authentic proof only. Do not infer privacy, retention or data-location guarantees from the audit's suggested wording.

Use a personal, empathetic first-person voice for the founder page: recognize that a guest's new question can be the host's repeated interruption, and frame the product as helping hosts care for guests without always being on the phone.

**Why:** The user requested a more emotional story that fellow hosts could identify with and explicitly approved that version as “perfetta”.

**How to apply:** Preserve this editorial direction when integrating the approved founder story; avoid replacing it with impersonal promotional copy.

Keep the homepage headline emotional rather than using it to explain the product category; the user prefers “Ore risparmiate. Ospiti felici. Sempre.”

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to restore the original headline because the gold badge already explains what NextSphere is, and the hero needs “un pò di emotività”.

**How to apply:** Preserve the emotional headline when improving SEO or clarity elsewhere; use the badge and supporting text for the product explanation.
