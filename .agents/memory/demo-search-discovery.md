---
name: Demo search discovery
description: SEO expectations when keeping the demo in an on-demand homepage overlay
---

Keep Google discovery claims separate from video-index eligibility. Video
sitemap entries and structured metadata do not guarantee a video search result
when the player exists only after an explicit interaction.

**Why:** The homepage demo intentionally loads no MP4 before opening. The SEO
update preserved that approved viewing flow rather than creating a watch page
or embedding an always-present video just for crawlers.

**How to apply:** Preserve on-demand loading when updating metadata; do not add
a fictitious demo URL to the sitemap. If video-result eligibility becomes an
explicit goal, explain the prominence/crawler-access tradeoff and agree any
separate watch-page scope before changing the current experience.
